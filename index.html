How interchanges work: overhead signs and the GPS tell you which lane to use. A yellow EXIT ONLY lane opens on the right, or a lane on the left for left-side connectors. Be in that lane when it splits off. Where a freeway ends, your lane decides which way you go. Miss it and the GPS reroutes you.
